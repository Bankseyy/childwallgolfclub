01 · Established 1912

The Course

James Braid parkland · Par 72 · 6,015 yards off the yellows

Braid laid the course out through mature oak and beech and barely moved a tree. It is not the longest test in Merseyside and it was never meant to be — position beats power on almost every hole, and the greens run quicker and slope harder than they look from the fairway.

Sand-banded fairways keep trolleys out through most of the winter, and membership carries reduced green fees at close to three hundred Braid courses across Britain and Ireland.

The test

Tree-lined, undulating greens, and a closing stretch that has decided more club championships than the card suggests.

Practice

Short game area, putting green and nets, with the professional taking lessons daily and fitting by appointment.

Condition

Sand-banded fairways, drainage worked hole by hole, and greens cut and rolled through the season.

Dress Code

Golf style throughout · members and visitors alike

Acceptable

  • All golf style clothing
  • Tailored shorts, just above the knee
  • Smart shorts and skirts, just above the knee
  • Golf caps and hats

Footwear

  • Golf shoes, rubber or metal spikes in good condition
  • Juniors under 16 may wear clean trainers
  • Clean shoes or change before the changing rooms

Not permitted

  • Denim, cargo trousers and shorts
  • Sports and beach shorts, tracksuits
  • Football shirts, vests, sleeveless or collarless shirts
  • Trainers, torn clothing, no socks
