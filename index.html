01 · Established 1912

The Course

James Braid parkland · Par 72 · 6568 yards

Childwall is a testing 6568 yard parkland course which demands accuracy from the tee. It presents a very tough test of golf. The course is not undulating and is very pleasant to walk.

The course design was based on the recommendation of James Braid when the Club moved to its present excellent site at Gateacre in 1938

PRACTICE

Short game practice area, chipping green, putting green and nets. Professional lessons available with a well-stocked pro shop.

THE COURSE

Tree-lined fairways, bunkers left and right, accuracy is a must. Undulating greens will test all of your skills

THE 19TH HOLE

Immaculate clubhouse serving excellent food in a friendly, hoispitable atmosphere. Designed by Alderman Shennan who subsequently designed the Royal Birkdale Clubhouse.
